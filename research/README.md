# Research Workspace

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Purpose:** Future home for public-safe research that informs proposals without silently becoming company policy or a marketable offer.

## Directory Rules

- Every future research note should identify its question, scope, source, publication or access date, jurisdiction or market, limitations, and reviewer.
- Separate external facts, observations, assumptions, options, recommendations, and owner decisions.
- Verify freshness when tools, prices, laws, regulations, standards, platforms, market conditions, or partner information may have changed.
- Use research to update the appropriate decision, risk, capability, tool, asset, service, or partner record only through its governance process.
- Respect the WIP policy: research into a future capability requires owner authorization when it would become the secondary capability-development track.
- Do not store private interview identities, supplier contacts, confidential quotes, credentials, copyrighted source copies, or sensitive commercial intelligence here.
- Research, course completion, tool access, travel, or a new contact does not validate capability or authorize public sale.

## Activation

This workspace becomes operational only for a bounded, owner-authorized research question. Its existence does not begin a company stage, validate a skill, approve a partner, purchase a tool, or activate a service.

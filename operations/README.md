# Operations Workspace

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Purpose:** Future home for public-safe operating architecture, controls, and approved procedural indexes.

## Directory Rules

- Treat [internal operations](../docs/16-internal-operations.md), the [SOP library](../docs/17-sop-library.md), information-handling rules, tool-adoption policy, and risk controls as governing sources.
- Standardize a real, observed process before selecting software or automation.
- Separate roles and functions from people; do not invent employees or assignments.
- Keep confidential SOPs, credentials, client records, contracts, invoices, payments, accounting data, vendor records, partner details, and security configuration in approved private systems.
- Link public-safe procedures to owner decisions, versions, dependencies, evidence, and review triggers.
- Do not infer that a tool, template, or documented process is operational or effective without acceptance and verification.

## Activation

This workspace becomes operational only after explicit owner authorization for the relevant delivery or operations stage. Its existence does not approve a system, tool, SOP, role, automation, or data store.

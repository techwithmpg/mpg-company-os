# Pricing and Commercial Model

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Document:** Pricing and Commercial Model
- **Stage:** Stage 2 — Services, Offers and Pricing
- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Last Updated:** 2026-08-28
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Authority:** Framework only; no prices or commercial terms are approved here

## Purpose

Define the questions and evidence needed for MPG to create repeatable, defensible commercial models for delivery-ready services. The framework prevents arbitrary pricing, hidden delivery costs, and the use of one pricing method across fundamentally different work. It does not price or launch any service.

## Questions This Stage Must Answer

### Pricing architecture

- What is the cost-based floor after direct delivery effort, founder or team time, overhead allocation, rework allowance, third-party costs, payment costs, and appropriate contingency are included?
- Which genuinely comparable market references are relevant by customer type, geography, scope, quality level, and delivery model?
- What customer value may reasonably inform price without relying on unprovable benefit claims?
- How will complexity, uncertainty, technical risk, urgency, revision load, delivery time, and support burden change the model?
- Which assumptions must be confirmed before a quote is valid?
- Which services suit fixed scope, time-based, milestone, retainer, maintenance, commission, or another approved structure?
- When should a custom business system require discovery and estimation rather than reuse a simple website price?

### Commercial controls

- What payment structure is appropriate: deposit, milestones, final payment, recurring billing, or another approved arrangement?
- What is included, excluded, client-supplied, and billable through a change request?
- How will margin be calculated and reviewed without concealing the real delivery effort?
- How will discounts, concessions, refunds, cancellations, late payment, and quote expiry be governed?
- How will taxes, fees, currency, exchange-rate exposure, and market differences be verified for the relevant jurisdiction?
- How will software, hosting, licenses, travel, media spend, partner work, freight, duties, and other pass-through costs be disclosed?
- For maintenance or retainers, what capacity, response, rollover, renewal, and exit boundaries are sustainable?
- For commissions or partner-dependent work, what disclosure, calculation, collection, liability, and conflict controls are required?

## Required Inputs

- An owner-authorized service definition with target customer, outcome, scope boundaries, delivery mode, and lifecycle status.
- Evidence from the customer, capability, tools, assets, process, quality, capacity, proof, partner, risk, and marketing readiness reviews.
- A documented delivery workflow and a realistic effort estimate.
- Verified direct, indirect, third-party, partner, maintenance, and support cost assumptions.
- Relevant market research with source dates, geography, and comparability limits.
- Applicable accounting, tax, legal, payment-provider, and currency guidance from qualified sources where required.
- Current capacity constraints and change-request policy.

## Required Outputs

- A pricing-method record for each eligible service, including assumptions, cost floor, margin logic, risk treatment, and review date.
- A reusable estimation worksheet and quote decision rules.
- Approved inclusion, exclusion, revision, change-request, payment, support, maintenance, and pass-through-cost rules.
- A market-reference record that distinguishes observations from owner decisions.
- An approval path for exceptions, discounts, partner costs, commissions, and material quote changes.
- Public-safe offer language that states commercial boundaries truthfully without exposing sensitive internal economics.

## Activation Gate

This framework may be activated only after Stage 1 is accepted and the MPG Founder explicitly authorizes Stage 2. A pricing method may be approved only for a sufficiently defined service with verified inputs. Pricing approval does not by itself make a service ACTIVE or publicly marketable; the full service readiness gates and explicit marketing approval remain required.

## Dependencies

- Service catalogue and service registry.
- Service lifecycle and service launch gates.
- Capability, tool, asset, and partner registries.
- Delivery, quality, risk, information-handling, and partner-governance policies.
- Later sales, quotation, contract, change-request, and accounting processes.

## What Must Not Yet Be Assumed

- No final price, currency, minimum, margin, deposit, fee, tax treatment, discount, retainer, maintenance rate, or commission is approved.
- No historical cost, market position, customer willingness to pay, delivery speed, or support load is known unless evidence is recorded.
- No partner cost, supplier quote, legal term, or tax treatment is current until verified.
- No single model is presumed suitable for websites, custom systems, automation, media, and trade.
- A price in a document, registry, or quote does not authorize public sale.

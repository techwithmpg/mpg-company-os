**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

## Purpose

Provide a disciplined structure for defining MPG's evidence-backed company positioning, message system, founder/company relationship, and brand expression after the owner accepts Stage 0 and explicitly activates the relevant Stage 1 work. The framework must turn the accepted Build/Grow/Connect architecture into clear communication without treating strategic domains as active services.

## Questions the stage must answer

- What real customer problem and company role should MPG be known for first?
- What is MPG's defensible positioning at its current maturity and evidence level?
- What promise can MPG make consistently without implying unsupported scale, capability, or results?
- Which values and operating beliefs are observable in decisions and delivery rather than aspirational slogans?
- How should the master brand and the BUILD, GROW, and CONNECT pillars appear across company communications?
- How should MPG explain broad long-term direction while keeping Professional Business Websites as the narrow commercial priority?
- Which messages belong to the MPG company brand and which belong to Malcom Gwanmesia's founder brand?
- What founder story is relevant, accurate, and proportionate without turning every learning area into an expertise claim?
- What tone and language best fit the selected customer and market context?
- What proof supports each material claim, and which claims must remain prohibited or qualified?
- Should `Build. Grow. Connect.` be retained, revised, replaced, or frozen after testing?
- How should active-offer, capability-journey, and thought-leadership content remain visibly distinct?
- What naming rules are needed for services, capability areas, content, and future divisions?
- How will the brand remain credible as capability grows without frequent unsupported repositioning?

## Required inputs

- Accepted decisions in `docs/02-decision-register.md`.
- Accepted architecture and boundaries in `docs/03-brand-architecture.md`.
- Current stage, commercial priority, and WIP limits in `docs/01-project-status.md`.
- Evidence-backed customer and market research from the activated market-discovery work.
- Current service, capability, tool, asset, proof, and capacity records.
- Applicable public-claims, quality, information-handling, and risk policies.
- Verified founder background and owner-provided company intent that are approved for public use.
- Competitive and category research that is current, sourced, and relevant to the chosen market.
- Any verified naming, trademark, legal, linguistic, or cultural checks required for the intended use.

## Required outputs

- An owner-reviewed positioning statement with its evidence and scope limits.
- A concise company narrative that distinguishes current capability from long-term direction.
- A message hierarchy for MPG, BUILD, GROW, CONNECT, and current approved offers.
- Explicit founder-brand and company-brand roles, overlap rules, and escalation rules.
- A truthful brand-promise and claims matrix linking each material claim to evidence.
- Voice and language guidance appropriate to the selected audience and context.
- A recommendation and recorded owner decision for `Build. Grow. Connect.`.
- Naming and architecture-usage rules that do not imply active or staffed divisions.
- Communication rules for active offers, capability journeys, and thought leadership.
- A list of unresolved questions, evidence gaps, prohibited assumptions, and later-stage dependencies.
- Decision-register entries showing which outputs are `PROPOSED`, `RECOMMENDED`, or `ACCEPTED`.

## Activation gate

- The MPG Founder has explicitly accepted Stage 0.
- The MPG Founder has explicitly authorized the relevant Stage 1 brand-strategy work.
- Required customer, capability, and proof inputs are available or clearly marked as gaps.
- The work has an owner-approved scope and does not displace the Professional Business Websites productization priority.
- Activation is recorded in the project status before substantive strategy decisions are treated as current work.

Completion of this framework is not activation. Brand outputs remain `PROPOSED` or `RECOMMENDED` until the owner explicitly accepts them.

## What must NOT yet be assumed

- That Stage 1 is active merely because Stage 0 has been accepted.
- That `Build. Grow. Connect.` is the final tagline.
- That strategic domains are active services, staffed divisions, or separate legal entities.
- That MPG has a validated target segment, positioning, promise, values, voice, or channel strategy.
- That founder learning proves professional proficiency or company delivery readiness.
- That named customers, results, testimonials, partners, certifications, locations, or infrastructure exist.
- That every long-term capability belongs in public-facing navigation or marketing.
- That visual identity, trademark availability, naming clearance, or legal protection has been established.
- That a brand decision authorizes service launch, pricing, marketing approval, or a later stage.

# Sales System

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Document:** Sales System
- **Stage:** Stage 3 — Sales System
- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Last Updated:** 2026-08-28
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Authority:** Framework only; no CRM or live sales process is approved here

## Purpose

Define a capability-aware sales flow that helps MPG pursue suitable work and decline, defer, or redesign work it cannot deliver reliably. The intended flow is Lead → Qualification → Discovery → Solution fit → Scope → Proposal → Quote → Contract → Deposit → Delivery handoff → Upsell or retention.

## Questions This Stage Must Answer

### Flow and stage exits

- What creates a lead, who may record it, and what minimum public-safe information is needed?
- What evidence allows a lead to move from qualification into discovery?
- What must discovery establish about the customer, problem, desired outcome, constraints, decision process, budget context, timing, risks, and data?
- How will solution fit be tested against an owner-authorized offer rather than a future strategic capability?
- What scope baseline, assumptions, exclusions, dependencies, client responsibilities, and acceptance criteria must exist before proposal and quote?
- When are proposal, quote, contract, deposit, and delivery handoff each complete?
- What conditions support an appropriate maintenance, follow-on, referral, or retention conversation after delivery?

### Qualification and protection

- Is the requested service ACTIVE with marketing approval, or is it an explicitly authorized controlled pilot?
- Does the opportunity match a defined customer and problem MPG has chosen to serve?
- Are required capabilities, tools, assets, processes, capacity, evidence, and approved partners available?
- Is the requested timeline feasible without compromising existing commitments?
- Are legal, compliance, information-security, payment, reputation, or partner risks understood?
- Are decision authority, procurement steps, commercial expectations, and client responsibilities sufficiently clear?
- Which conditions require decline, wait-listing, referral, paid discovery, narrower scope, partner review, or owner escalation?
- How will MPG avoid promising unsupported outcomes during sales conversations?

### Governance

- Which functions may approve scope, exceptions, discounts, partner involvement, non-standard terms, and pilots?
- How will proposal and quotation versions remain aligned?
- What record demonstrates that contract and required payment conditions were satisfied before delivery begins?
- What information may be stored in this public repository, and what must move to approved private storage?
- Which small set of measures would later show flow health without incentivizing low-quality leads?

## Required Inputs

- Market-approved service records or explicit owner authorization for a controlled pilot.
- Accepted customer, offer, pricing-method, capacity, and delivery definitions.
- Current capability, tool, asset, evidence, and partner records.
- Approved public claims, proposal and quotation outlines, contract or terms guidance, and payment rules.
- Information-handling, risk, quality, and partner-governance requirements.
- A verified route for private lead and client data before real records are stored.

## Required Outputs

- A documented sales pipeline with entry criteria, exit criteria, owner, evidence, and permitted actions for every stage.
- A qualification scorecard with hard-stop, escalation, defer, and decline rules.
- Discovery and solution-fit guides linked to service readiness and delivery capacity.
- Scope, proposal, quote, contract, deposit, and handoff controls with version ownership.
- A public-claims checklist for sales communication.
- A private-record specification and retention rules for leads and clients.
- A no-fit response and referral approach that does not imply an unverified partnership.

## Activation Gate

This framework may be activated only after Stage 2 is accepted and the MPG Founder explicitly authorizes Stage 3. Live solicitation must be limited to services that satisfy the publication gate. Any pilot path must be separately authorized, controlled, accurately described, and excluded from general public marketing unless its lifecycle and approval state permit it.

## Dependencies

- Customer and market definition.
- Service registry, lifecycle, readiness gates, and public-claims policy.
- Approved pricing method and offer boundaries.
- Delivery capacity, risk, partner, quality, and information-handling controls.
- Proposal, quotation, discovery, and decision-record templates.

## What Must Not Yet Be Assumed

- No CRM, pipeline stages in software, lead source, platform, sales target, conversion rate, or forecast is approved.
- No lead is qualified merely because it has budget or urgency.
- No proposal, quote, contract, deposit percentage, or sales script is approved by this framework.
- No future service may be sold through custom wording that bypasses its lifecycle or marketing approval.
- No customer or opportunity data belongs in this public repository.
- A signed sale must not override capability, capacity, legal, partner, or delivery safeguards.

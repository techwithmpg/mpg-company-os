## Purpose

Define how MPG will identify, investigate, compare, and select evidence-backed customer segments and market opportunities before converting a strategic capability into a commercial offer. The immediate later-stage use is to establish who, if anyone, Professional Business Websites should serve first and which problems MPG can solve reliably.

## Questions the stage must answer

- Which customer groups experience a problem that fits MPG's current and near-term capability?
- Who is the buyer, user, approver, payer, and delivery collaborator for each candidate segment?
- What event or condition creates urgency to solve the problem?
- How do customers currently solve it, and what is inadequate about the alternatives?
- What evidence demonstrates demand rather than founder interest or anecdote?
- What outcomes do customers value, and how do they describe those outcomes in their own language?
- Which constraints affect purchase and delivery, including budget logic, decision cycles, content readiness, technology, geography, regulation, accessibility, and support expectations?
- What trust, proof, experience, or relationship signals are required before a buyer will engage MPG?
- Which projects should MPG decline because of scope, risk, capability, capacity, economics, ethics, jurisdiction, or partner dependency?
- Which customer segments best fit the current Professional Business Websites priority?
- Within those segments, which website types and business problems are sufficiently repeatable to productize?
- What research sample and evidence threshold are sufficient for an initial decision?
- Which assumptions remain hypotheses, and what would disprove them?
- How might later BUILD, GROW, or CONNECT opportunities be recorded without distracting from the active commercial track?

## Required inputs

- Current stage, priority, and WIP limits from `docs/01-project-status.md`.
- Accepted commercial sequence and claim constraints from `docs/02-decision-register.md`.
- Accepted architecture from `docs/03-brand-architecture.md`.
- Current service, capability, asset, tool, capacity, and evidence records.
- Structured founder observations clearly labelled as observations rather than market facts.
- Current primary research such as interviews, discovery conversations, or observed workflows, with private data stored outside this public repository.
- Current secondary research from authoritative, cited sources where market freshness matters.
- Competitive and alternative-solution review focused on customer choices rather than imitation.
- Applicable legal, compliance, risk, information-handling, and public-claims constraints.

## Required outputs

- A public-safe research log that separates evidence, inference, and assumption.
- Candidate customer segments with explicit problem statements and evidence strength.
- Buyer, user, approver, and stakeholder roles for each serious candidate.
- Segment comparison criteria aligned with capability, delivery risk, economics, capacity, proof, and strategic fit.
- An initial ideal-customer profile and exclusion profile, each marked with its decision state.
- Customer language, desired outcomes, objections, trust requirements, and buying constraints supported by research.
- A prioritized recommendation for the first website-service segment, or a finding that evidence is insufficient.
- Disqualification and referral criteria that prevent MPG from accepting unsuitable projects.
- Unresolved questions and a plan to validate or reject remaining hypotheses.
- Owner decisions recorded in the decision register before recommendations govern brand, service, pricing, sales, or website work.

## Activation gate

- The MPG Founder has explicitly accepted Stage 0.
- The MPG Founder has authorized the relevant Stage 1 customer/market work.
- A public-safe research method and private-data handling method are agreed.
- Research scope is narrow enough to support the Professional Business Websites priority without activating multiple productization tracks.
- Relevant capability and capacity records are available or explicitly unknown.
- Activation is recorded in project status before research outputs are treated as current-stage decisions.

Research activity alone does not activate a service. Customer/market outputs remain `PROPOSED` or `RECOMMENDED` until owner acceptance.

## What must NOT yet be assumed

- That Stage 1 is active or customer/market work has owner authorization.
- That a target customer, industry, geography, buyer role, problem, budget, or channel has been validated.
- That founder familiarity with a market proves demand or fit.
- That an addressable market estimate proves an attainable market for MPG.
- That interest, survey responses, social engagement, or competitor activity equals purchase intent.
- That Professional Business Websites are already ready, priced, active, or market-approved.
- That all website projects or customer segments are suitable for MPG.
- That future BUILD, GROW, or CONNECT customers should be pursued concurrently.
- That private interview notes, contact details, or commercially sensitive research may be committed here.
- That a customer-segment decision automatically approves an offer, price, campaign, public claim, or website page.

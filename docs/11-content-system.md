# Content System

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Document:** Content System
- **Stage:** Stage 6 — Marketing and Content Engine
- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Last Updated:** 2026-08-28
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Authority:** Framework only; no editorial plan or publishing cadence is approved here

## Purpose

Define how MPG may research, create, verify, approve, publish, reuse, correct, measure, and archive public content. The system must let the company and founder build trust while keeping active offers, learning journeys, and thought leadership visibly distinct.

## Questions This Stage Must Answer

### Content classification and intent

- Is the item company content or founder content?
- Is it active-offer content, capability-journey content, or thought leadership?
- What audience question, business purpose, or documented learning does it address?
- If it names a service, does the authoritative catalogue permit a sales claim and call to action?
- If it discusses a future capability, does the wording unmistakably describe research, learning, testing, or planning?

### Evidence and editorial control

- What first-hand evidence, dated source, approved asset, or expert review supports each material claim?
- Which claims concern results, customers, partners, qualifications, law, finance, security, health, or another high-risk subject?
- What permissions and usage rights cover names, logos, quotations, screenshots, photography, music, data, or project details?
- Who drafts, fact-checks, brand-checks, approves, publishes, corrects, and retires content?
- How will revisions preserve the source, approval, version, date, and reason for change?

### Production and distribution

- Which reusable content brief captures audience, intent, category, evidence, message, format, call to action, and risk?
- What minimum workflow can MPG sustain without distracting from the primary commercial build track?
- How should one verified source be adapted to different formats without losing its context or qualification?
- Which accessibility, format, link, metadata, and platform checks are required?
- How will comments, enquiries, errors, outdated claims, and takedown requests be handled?

### Learning and measurement

- What question will each content effort help answer?
- Which observable measures can be collected reliably and lawfully?
- How will MPG distinguish attention, engagement, enquiry, qualified lead, and attributable commercial outcome?
- When should a topic, format, channel, or series be continued, changed, paused, corrected, or archived?

## Required Inputs

- Accepted brand voice, message architecture, visual identity, and audience research.
- Market-approved service data for active-offer content.
- Public-claims policy, capability status, source material, evidence, and permissions.
- Approved asset records and platform-specific research.
- Publishing capacity, review roles, information-handling rules, and measurement definitions.

## Required Outputs

- A content taxonomy covering company or founder ownership and all three communication categories.
- A content brief, evidence log, claims checklist, permissions check, and approval workflow.
- An editorial backlog and calendar method that records priority and capacity without inventing a required cadence.
- Channel adaptation, accessibility, correction, versioning, archival, and asset-reuse procedures.
- A public-safe content register linking each item to its evidence, approval, publication location, status, and review date.
- Measurement definitions that can later connect content to qualified enquiries where evidence permits.

## Activation Gate

This framework may be activated with the Marketing System only after Stage 5 and the prerequisite brand and offer decisions are accepted and the MPG Founder explicitly authorizes Stage 6. No item may be published as active-offer content unless the relevant service passes the publication gate. High-risk claims or third-party materials require the appropriate evidence, permission, and review before publication.

## Dependencies

- Marketing system, brand strategy, visual identity, and customer research.
- Service registry and generated marketable-service catalogue.
- Public-claims, information-handling, WIP, risk, and quality policies.
- Asset registry, case-study permission process, and later metrics catalogue.

## What Must Not Yet Be Assumed

- No editorial themes, voice rules, formats, content pillars, platform mix, cadence, budget, approval roles, or targets are final.
- No social account, audience, mailing list, content library, image right, or publication capability is presumed available.
- Course completion, experiments, or research are not proof of professional service capability.
- Engagement does not equal qualified demand, attribution, or revenue.
- Publicly available material is not automatically licensed for reuse.
- This framework does not authorize marketing a planned service.

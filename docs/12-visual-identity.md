# Visual Identity

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Document:** Visual Identity
- **Stage:** Stage 5 — Visual Identity and Design System
- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Last Updated:** 2026-08-28
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Authority:** Framework only; no visual identity choice is approved here

## Purpose

Define the decisions, evidence, assets, and controls needed for a coherent, accessible MPG visual identity. The future system must express MPG as the company while allowing the founder brand and the Build, Grow, and Connect architecture to relate clearly without inventing separate businesses or finalizing unapproved brand elements.

## Questions This Stage Must Answer

### Identity architecture

- What visual relationship should exist between MPG, Malcom Gwanmesia as founder brand, MPG Technologies, MPG Media, and MPG Trade & Distribution?
- Which elements are shared, which may vary by division, and how will variations avoid fragmenting recognition?
- How should the working Build. Grow. Connect. idea appear, if at all, before or after it is explicitly approved as a tagline?
- What qualities should the identity communicate to the accepted audience, and what research supports those qualities?

### Core system

- What primary, secondary, and compact logo forms are needed, and what clear-space, minimum-size, background, and misuse rules protect legibility?
- Which primary, supporting, accent, and neutral colors meet functional and accessibility needs?
- Which typography choices are legible, appropriately licensed, technically available, and suitable across web, documents, presentations, and social formats?
- What hierarchy, spacing, grid, shape, line, motion, and component principles create consistency?
- What photography, illustration, data-visualization, icon, and screenshot styles fit truthful company and founder communication?
- Which design tokens should express approved color, typography, spacing, elevation, radius, and state decisions across digital products?

### Governance and quality

- Who may create, approve, release, modify, and retire identity assets?
- Which master formats, exports, file names, version records, rights records, and storage locations are required?
- Which checks cover contrast, text size, focus states, color independence, alt text, scaling, print reproduction, and responsive use?
- How will materials be reviewed for consistency without blocking necessary functional variation?
- What separates an exploratory concept from an approved production asset?

## Required Inputs

- Accepted company, founder, brand-architecture, audience, positioning, and naming decisions.
- Evidence from customer and stakeholder research relevant to perception and usability.
- A complete inventory of existing marks, images, fonts, templates, handles, and rights, with unknown items recorded as unknown.
- Required use cases, production constraints, accessibility requirements, and target media.
- Owner-approved design brief and budget or resource constraints.

## Required Outputs

- An approved visual-identity brief and recorded owner decisions.
- A rights-cleared logo suite with usage, clear-space, minimum-size, background, and misuse rules.
- Approved accessible color and typography specifications.
- Photography, illustration, iconography, layout, motion, and data-visualization guidance where needed.
- A versioned token set and representative component or template applications.
- Asset naming, format, storage, licensing, approval, release, and retirement rules.
- A visual QA checklist covering accessibility and the intended media.

## Activation Gate

This framework may be activated only after Stage 4 and the required Stage 1 company and brand foundations are accepted and the MPG Founder explicitly authorizes Stage 5. Concepts remain proposals until the owner records approval. An approved identity does not make any service, division, claim, or tagline market-approved.

## Dependencies

- Brand architecture, brand strategy, customer and market framework, and decision register.
- Asset registry and information-handling policy.
- Public-claims, quality, and risk policies.
- Later marketing, content, website, presentation, and document systems.

## What Must Not Yet Be Assumed

- No logo, symbol, color, typeface, imagery style, icon style, token, template, division treatment, or final tagline is approved.
- No existing asset is assumed to be original, licensed, current, accessible, or production-ready until verified.
- Build, Grow, and Connect do not automatically require separate logos or color systems.
- A concept generated by a person or tool is not cleared for use without rights and quality review.
- Visual polish is not proof of company scale, capability, customers, partnerships, or results.

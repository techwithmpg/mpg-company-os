# Internal Operations

- **Document:** Internal Operations
- **Stage:** Stage 8 — Internal Operations and Automation
- **Owner:** MPG Founder

- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Authority:** Framework only; no operational platform or tool purchase is approved here

## Purpose

Define the future operational architecture MPG needs to manage commercial work safely and consistently as real demand develops. It covers functions and records for CRM, invoicing, accounting, document storage, project management, contracts, payments, analytics, client records, vendor records, asset management, and internal automation without preselecting or buying a complete software stack.

## Questions This Stage Must Answer

### Process and source of truth

- Which recurring process, decision, handoff, record, or control creates a genuine operational need?
- What is the authoritative source for lead, client, project, contract, invoice, payment, vendor, partner, asset, service, and performance information?
- Which functions create, review, approve, use, correct, retain, export, and delete each record?
- How will duplicate records, version conflicts, missing approvals, and disconnected tools be prevented?

### Information and access

- Which information is public, internal, confidential, restricted, legally retained, or unsuitable for this repository?
- Which approved private storage is required before real client, financial, contract, payment, vendor, or partner records exist?
- What access, least-privilege, authentication, backup, recovery, retention, deletion, audit, offboarding, and incident requirements apply?
- How will ownership and access remain with MPG or the appropriate client if a person, contractor, tool, or vendor changes?

### Tool and workflow choices

- Can the process be standardized before a tool is selected?
- What minimum capability is required, and which candidate meets it at acceptable total cost, data risk, integration effort, usability, portability, and support burden?
- Which system boundaries and integrations are necessary, and where would manual handoffs be safer or simpler?
- What migration, export, vendor-exit, business-continuity, and fallback plan is required?
- Which internal automations have a stable process, clear benefit, monitoring, human fallback, and maintenance owner?

### Operating control

- What approval levels apply to contracts, quotes, expenses, invoices, payments, refunds, access, vendors, and exceptions?
- What recurring review identifies overdue work, cash obligations, delivery risk, client issues, access problems, and vendor dependency?
- What evidence demonstrates that a process is being followed and remains useful?
- When does demand justify a contractor, dedicated role, additional system, or more formal control?

## Required Inputs

- Accepted service, sales, delivery, pricing, quality, information, risk, and role definitions.
- Real process observations and operational pain evidence.
- Data inventory, classification, retention, access, backup, recovery, and legal requirements.
- Tool and vendor evaluations with costs, ownership, export, security, privacy, and exit considerations.
- Current capacity, transaction volume, control risks, and private-storage readiness.

## Required Outputs

- An operating-process map and source-of-truth map for each approved record type.
- A data-classification, access, retention, backup, recovery, offboarding, and incident model.
- Minimum requirements and recorded decisions for CRM, finance, document, project, contract, payment, analytics, vendor, client, and asset systems as actually needed.
- Role-based approval, segregation, exception, reconciliation, and review controls.
- Tool implementation, migration, integration, training, support, continuity, and exit plans.
- A prioritized automation candidate list that passes the separate technology and automation safeguards.

## Activation Gate

This framework may be activated only after Stage 7 is accepted, the MPG Founder explicitly authorizes Stage 8, and predecessor processes are sufficiently stable to systematize. A tool may be adopted only through the tool-adoption policy for a defined need with an approved owner, cost, data treatment, implementation plan, and exit route. Private operational records require approved private storage before use.

## Dependencies

- Sales, delivery, pricing, technology and automation, metrics, and SOP frameworks.
- Information-handling, tool-adoption, risk, quality, partner, and company-growth policies.
- Service, capability, tool, asset, and partner registries.
- Appropriate accounting, tax, legal, privacy, security, and payment guidance.

## What Must Not Yet Be Assumed

- No CRM, accounting package, project manager, storage provider, contract platform, payment processor, analytics product, or automation tool is selected or required.
- No account, integration, subscription, budget, data migration, employee, contractor, volume, or operating metric exists unless evidenced.
- A popular tool is not automatically suitable.
- One person covering several functions does not remove the need to identify approval and conflict risks.
- Confidential client, financial, contract, supplier, vendor, partner, credential, or payment information must not be stored in this public repository.

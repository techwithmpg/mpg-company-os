## Charter statement

MPG Company OS exists to govern how MPG develops from a founder-led company into a credible, diversified organization without overclaiming, scattering effort, launching prematurely, or losing accepted decisions.

The repository is the durable public-safe source of truth for company direction, capability readiness, service governance, stage frameworks, and auditable operational design. It is not merely documentation and it is not merely the MPG website. The website is a future Stage 7 output governed by this system.

## Intended outcome

The Company OS must make it possible for MPG to:

- plan long-term capability areas without advertising them as current services;
- develop one primary commercial capability sequentially;
- assess skills, tools, assets, processes, economics, capacity, proof, compliance, and partners before launch;
- record owner decisions durably and retain superseded history;
- derive future public offers only from approved service records;
- separate founder development from company productization;
- keep public claims aligned with evidence; and
- expand organizational systems as real demand and capability expand.

Success is not measured by how large MPG appears. It is measured by how reliably the system protects focus, truth, readiness, quality, and owner authority.

## Current mandate

**Stage 0 — Governance and Source of Truth is ACCEPTED** as the governing Company OS baseline under `MPG-DEC-032`. **Stage 1 — Company and Brand Foundation is ACTIVE** under `MPG-DEC-033` and operates within the architecture and controls established by Stage 0. Stage 2 and all later stages remain inactive pending their own explicit owner decisions.

The current commercial priority is **Professional Business Websites**. The primary build track is website service productization. The secondary capability-development track remains **UNALLOCATED / owner decision required**.

## Scope

Stage 0 includes:

- project charter, current status, decision register, and accepted brand architecture;
- source-of-truth and decision-state rules;
- service lifecycle, launch readiness, publication approval, and work-in-progress controls;
- public claims, partner, quality, risk, information, growth, and tool governance;
- public-safe service, capability, tool, asset, and partner registries;
- reusable governance and operational templates;
- framework documents for future stages that state questions rather than invent answers;
- validation and generation logic connecting market-approved records to a future website catalogue;
- a readiness-audit backlog for Professional Business Websites;
- a future Client Project Readiness & Dependency Detection requirement, without implementing later-stage sales or delivery workflows; and
- basic repository validation automation.

## Out of scope at Stage 0

Stage 0 does not:

- finalize brand positioning, brand voice, visual identity, or the tagline;
- choose final customer segments without research;
- set final prices, margins, financial targets, or contract terms;
- launch, market, or imply availability of any service;
- build the MPG website or implement a CRM, accounting, sales, or automation platform;
- claim employees, customers, testimonials, certifications, partners, locations, or business results;
- validate founder proficiency without evidence;
- treat a founder capability declaration as evidence validation or service readiness;
- approve legal, licensing, regulatory, tax, insurance, privacy, or security compliance; or
- store confidential company, client, supplier, partner, or financial records in this public repository.

## Accepted operating principles

1. **Plan broadly; sell narrowly.** Strategic intent may precede commercial readiness.
2. **Build sequentially.** One major new service family is productized at a time by default.
3. **Evidence over hype.** Unknown facts remain unknown; no scale or capability is fabricated.
4. **Capability before commercialization.** Skills alone are insufficient; delivery also depends on tools, assets, process, QA, economics, capacity, proof, compliance, and partners.
5. **Owner approval before publication.** A service is publicly marketable only when it is `ACTIVE` and `marketingApproved = true`.
6. **Fewer things, done well.** MPG should prefer doing fewer things extremely well over offering many things poorly.
7. **Public brand follows delivery reality.** Marketing must never run ahead of dependable capability.
8. **Decisions are durable.** Recommendations do not become accepted silently; superseded decisions remain traceable.

## Company identity and working architecture

- **MPG** is the master company brand.
- **Malcom Gwanmesia** is the founder brand.
- **Build. Grow. Connect.** is a working brand idea, not a frozen final tagline.
- **MPG Technologies** owns the strategic **BUILD** pillar.
- **MPG Media** owns the strategic **GROW** pillar.
- **MPG Trade & Distribution** owns the strategic **CONNECT** pillar.

These pillars are capability domains. They do not establish active offers, staffed divisions, separate legal entities, licences, infrastructure, or partner relationships.

## Founder/company boundary

Malcom represents founder leadership, trust, expertise, learning, thinking, and relationships. MPG represents company systems, offers, processes, delivery, and commercial operations.

The founder may study, discuss, and declare practical ability in a wide set of subjects. The current owner declaration covers Web Development, Business Systems, Automation, and Mobile Applications. This declaration is a distinct source-of-truth dimension, not evidence maturity: granular capability records remain independently assessed, and MPG productizes and sells only services supported by readiness evidence and approval.

## Commercial build sequence

The accepted productization order is:

1. Professional business websites
2. Custom business systems
3. AI and workflow automation
4. Mobile applications
5. Digital marketing
6. Social media management
7. Trade, sourcing, and distribution

This order does not prohibit personal learning. It controls which company service family receives primary commercialization effort.

## Work-in-progress control

- One major new service family may be actively productized at a time by default.
- At most one meaningful future capability may be under structured learning, research, or incubation alongside it unless the owner explicitly authorizes otherwise.
- All other opportunities remain `IDEA`, `RESEARCH`, `PLANNED`, `BACKLOG`, or `PARKED` as applicable.

A tool purchase, course, experiment, or possible partner does not alter these limits.

## Service and publication governance

Service records use the lifecycle defined by the operating model. Movement toward `ACTIVE` requires evidence against the applicable launch gates and appropriate owner decisions.

Public offer content requires both:

1. `lifecycleStatus = ACTIVE`; and
2. `marketingApproved = true` by explicit owner authorization.

The internal service catalogue may contain future capabilities. The public catalogue must contain only records that pass both conditions.

## Authority model

Conflicts are resolved by:

1. latest explicit owner decision;
2. latest `ACCEPTED` or `FROZEN` repository documentation;
3. current project context; and
4. individual chat or conversation history.

The MPG Founder owns final company, stage, lifecycle-publication, and decision-state authority. Contributors and agents may research, propose, implement authorized work, and identify evidence. They may not infer acceptance.

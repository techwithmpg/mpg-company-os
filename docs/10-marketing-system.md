# Marketing System

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Document:** Marketing System
- **Stage:** Stage 6 — Marketing and Content Engine
- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Last Updated:** 2026-08-28
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Authority:** Framework only; no campaign, channel, or claim is approved here

## Purpose

Define a truthful marketing system that grows MPG's company brand and Malcom Gwanmesia's founder brand without converting strategic interests into unsupported offers. It separates company marketing, founder marketing, and capability-development communication while preserving evidence-over-hype.

## Questions This Stage Must Answer

### Company marketing

- Which audience, problem, and market-approved MPG offer is the communication about?
- What verified proof supports each service, quality, experience, outcome, partner, or capacity claim?
- What action may the audience take, and can MPG fulfill the resulting demand?
- How will company narrative distinguish active offers from broader Build, Grow, and Connect capability areas?
- How will individual MPG Media capabilities such as strategy, creative, copywriting, organic social, paid media, tracking, analytics, SEO, and conversion optimization be evaluated independently rather than bundled into an unsupported full-service claim?

### Founder marketing

- Which topics credibly reflect Malcom's leadership, journey, lessons, thinking, experience, and relationship building?
- How will personal observations be distinguished from MPG commitments and market-approved services?
- Which claims require evidence, qualification, permission, or professional review?
- How will the founder avoid presenting himself as simultaneously validated in every future MPG domain?

### Capability-development communication

- Is a post active-offer content, capability-journey content, or thought leadership?
- For capability-journey content, what wording clearly states that MPG is learning, researching, experimenting, or building rather than selling?
- For thought leadership, what sources and experience support the point without implying a commercial offer?
- What review is required before capability-development material links to a contact or sales action?

### Channel and campaign choices

- Where does the intended audience actually seek information, and what evidence supports using a channel?
- What role, resource requirement, content form, risk, and measurable learning does each candidate channel have?
- What is the minimum sustainable publishing and response workflow?
- How will claims, approvals, source material, reuse rights, accessibility, moderation, and corrections be governed?
- How will channel performance be interpreted without vanity metrics or invented attribution?

## Required Inputs

- Accepted company and founder brand foundations.
- Service registry and the authoritative marketable-service output.
- Verified customer research, offer boundaries, capacity, and public claims.
- Approved visual identity, messaging evidence, content permissions, and asset records.
- Current channel research with dates and source limitations.
- Risk, information-handling, legal or regulatory, and measurement requirements.

## Required Outputs

- Separate company, founder, and capability-development message architectures.
- A communication classification and approval workflow for active-offer, capability-journey, and thought-leadership content.
- Audience and channel decision records with evidence, owner decision, resource cost, and review date.
- Campaign briefs tied only to eligible offers, truthful proof, capacity, and a defined call to action.
- Claims, proof, permission, correction, and archival records.
- A small measurement specification linked to business questions rather than assumed targets.

## Activation Gate

This framework may be activated only after Stage 5 and the prerequisite brand, offer, and delivery foundations are accepted and the MPG Founder explicitly authorizes Stage 6. Active-offer marketing is permitted only for a service whose lifecycleStatus = ACTIVE and whose marketingApproved = true. Capability-journey and thought-leadership content must still pass public-claims and information-safety review.

## Dependencies

- Brand architecture, brand strategy, customer and market framework, and visual identity.
- Service catalogue, service registry, generated marketable-service output, and launch gates.
- Public-claims, evidence, information-handling, quality, risk, and WIP policies.
- Content system, asset registry, and later metrics framework.

## What Must Not Yet Be Assumed

- No positioning, message, slogan, proof point, campaign, channel, platform, cadence, audience size, budget, or result is approved.
- Build. Grow. Connect. remains a working brand idea, not a frozen tagline.
- Creating posts does not establish full-service marketing capability.
- Founder learning does not establish MPG service readiness.
- No customer, testimonial, project result, partnership, certification, team, office, or performance claim exists without evidence and permission.
- Being listed in the strategic architecture does not make a capability marketable.

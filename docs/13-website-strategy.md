# Website Strategy

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Document:** Website Strategy
- **Stage:** Stage 7 — MPG Website
- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Last Updated:** 2026-08-28
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Authority:** Framework only; no website scope, technology, or publication is approved here

## Purpose

Define a future MPG website that communicates the company truthfully and derives purchasable services only from governed, market-approved records. The website must support company narrative, active offers, capability areas, proof, founder context, insights, and contact without presenting every long-term ambition as available for sale.

## Questions This Stage Must Answer

### Information architecture

- What must a visitor understand about MPG, the founder, Build, Grow, and Connect without confusing divisions, capabilities, and offers?
- Which distinct destinations are needed for company narrative, active offers, case studies, capability areas, founder, insights, and contact?
- How will strategic capability pages explain direction or learning without a purchase claim or service call to action?
- What should the website show if no service is currently approved for automatic public publication?
- Which audience journeys and accessibility needs should shape navigation, search, page hierarchy, and calls to action?

### Authoritative service catalogue

- How will the website consume an approved service record or generated catalogue rather than a manually duplicated list?
- How will publication logic verify both lifecycle status ACTIVE and marketing approval true?
- Which fields may appear publicly, which require transformation, and which must never leave internal or private systems?
- How will a status or approval change remove or update an offer without leaving stale pages, navigation, metadata, structured data, or cached claims?
- Which automated and human tests will prove that non-approved strategic services cannot appear as purchasable offers?

### Content, proof, and enquiries

- Which claims, case studies, testimonials, customer names, images, and results have evidence and explicit public permission?
- How will the founder page distinguish personal journey and thought leadership from company delivery commitments?
- What contact or enquiry paths can MPG service securely and within capacity?
- What records, consent, spam protection, privacy notice, retention, and access controls are required for submitted data?

### Technical and release choices

- What content ownership, editing workflow, hosting, domain, analytics, search, security, privacy, performance, accessibility, backup, monitoring, and recovery requirements exist?
- Which technical approach best fits those requirements and MPG's validated maintenance capability?
- What environments, browser or device checks, redirects, metadata, error states, legal review, content approval, and rollback evidence are required before launch?
- Who owns ongoing content, dependency, security, catalogue, and availability maintenance?

## Required Inputs

- Accepted brand, audience, marketing, content, and visual-identity decisions.
- Valid service registry data and the generated marketable-services output.
- Approved public descriptions, claims, assets, calls to action, and case-study permissions.
- Verified domain, hosting, tool, capability, security, privacy, accessibility, analytics, support, and maintenance requirements.
- Capacity for enquiries and a private data-handling route.

## Required Outputs

- An approved website brief, audience journeys, sitemap, and content model.
- A catalogue integration contract naming the governed source, eligibility rule, permitted public fields, empty state, update behavior, and failure behavior.
- Wireframes or prototypes and an accessible design-system implementation plan.
- A page and asset inventory with owner, source, evidence, approval, and review date.
- Functional, content, catalogue-gate, accessibility, performance, security, privacy, analytics, backup, and rollback acceptance tests.
- A launch, maintenance, incident, correction, and retirement plan.

## Activation Gate

This framework may be activated only after Stage 6 and its prerequisite decisions are accepted and the MPG Founder explicitly authorizes Stage 7. Public launch requires approved content, operational ownership, risk review, and release evidence. An active service page may be published only when the authoritative service data satisfies lifecycleStatus = ACTIVE and marketingApproved = true; owner approval must not be inferred from website copy or code.

## Dependencies

- Service registry, marketable-service generator, validator, lifecycle, and launch gates.
- Brand, customer, visual identity, marketing, content, and public-claims frameworks.
- Asset, capability, tool, and partner records.
- Information handling, security, quality, risk, and later operational ownership.

## What Must Not Yet Be Assumed

- No sitemap, page, copy, design, domain, hosting provider, framework, CMS, analytics tool, form, integration, budget, launch date, or maintenance owner is approved.
- The website must not hard-code all strategic capabilities as available services.
- A service registry entry, capability area, or draft page is not evidence of market approval.
- No case study, customer logo, testimonial, result, partner, location, team, or contact detail may be invented.
- A live website does not by itself activate a service.

## Handling rule

Do not complete this record in the public repository. Store private contacts, IDs, licenses, contracts, bank details, references, non-public prices, and adverse findings in approved private storage. Use only a public-safe partner ID and status summary in `registry/partners.json`.

A candidate, contact, quotation, or trial is not an “official MPG partner” and does not authorize service marketing.

## Handling and qualification rule

Store a completed copy in approved private storage, not this public repository. Collect the minimum information needed; do not ask a client to paste passwords, private keys, payment credentials, or unnecessary personal data. Discovery tests fit—it is not a promise, quote, or acceptance of work.

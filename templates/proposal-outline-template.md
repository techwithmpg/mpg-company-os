## Use rule

Store completed proposals in approved private storage. An ordinary commercial proposal must map to an `ACTIVE` service with marketing approval and current capacity. A controlled pilot uses explicit pilot authorization and must not be described as an active offer. A proposal is not a contract, quote acceptance, or permission to begin work unless governing terms explicitly make it so.

## Rating definitions

**Probability:** `UNLIKELY`, `POSSIBLE`, `LIKELY`<br>
**Impact:** `LOW`, `MODERATE`, `HIGH`, `SEVERE`

Every rating needs a short evidence-based rationale. Do not multiply invented numbers. Residual ratings describe exposure after controls, not desired outcomes.

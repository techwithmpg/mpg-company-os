## Publication control

An offer sheet may be drafted internally before launch, but it must be labelled **INTERNAL DRAFT — NOT AN ACTIVE OFFER** unless the linked service is `ACTIVE` and marketing approval is `true`. `Publicly marketable` must equal that predicate as a derived field, not a third approval. Do not invent proof, price, capacity, partner relationships, or urgency.

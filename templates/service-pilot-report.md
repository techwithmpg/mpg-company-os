## Pilot rule

A pilot is a deliberately bounded learning and validation exercise, not a quiet commercial launch. Participation, payment, or a successful outcome does not make the service `ACTIVE` and does not create marketing approval. Use client/participant identifiers and evidence only in approved private storage unless explicit publication permission exists.

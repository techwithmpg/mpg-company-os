# Marketing Workspace

**STATUS: FRAMEWORK — NOT YET ACTIVATED**

- **Owner:** MPG Founder
- **Public Safe:** Yes
- **Purpose:** Future home for approved, public-safe MPG marketing and content system materials.

## Directory Rules

- Treat the [marketing system](../docs/10-marketing-system.md), [content system](../docs/11-content-system.md), brand sources, public-claims policy, and generated marketable-service catalogue as governing sources.
- Label work as company or founder content and as active-offer, capability-journey, or thought-leadership content.
- Active-offer material may reference only services whose authoritative record permits public marketing.
- Capability-journey language must say when MPG or Malcom is learning, researching, testing, or building; it must not imply an available service.
- Record evidence, source dates, permissions, approval, version, and correction history for future public material.
- Never fabricate customers, proof, results, testimonials, partnerships, experience, scale, or metrics.
- Do not assume every platform, channel, cadence, or media capability is needed.

## Activation

This workspace becomes operational only after explicit owner authorization for Stage 6 and acceptance of its dependencies. Its existence does not approve a campaign, channel, claim, asset, or service.

## Directory Rules

- Treat the [sales system](../docs/08-sales-system.md), service registry, approved pricing method, launch gates, and public-claims policy as governing sources.
- Sales materials may solicit only services that satisfy the publication gate; an owner-authorized controlled pilot must remain accurately described and separately governed.
- Keep qualification, discovery, scope, proposal, quote, contract, deposit, and delivery-handoff controls aligned.
- Mark drafts and recommendations clearly; do not treat a template as an accepted commercial term.
- Never store lead names, contact details, discovery notes, proposals, contracts, payment records, credentials, or other client data in this public repository.
- Do not implement or assume a CRM until Stage 3 is authorized and a defined operational need passes tool review.

## Activation

This workspace becomes operational only after explicit owner authorization for Stage 3. Its existence does not authorize solicitation, pricing, discounting, contracting, or acceptance of work beyond MPG's capability or capacity.
